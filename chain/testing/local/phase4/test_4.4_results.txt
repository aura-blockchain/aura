Step 5: Downtime Slashing Mechanics
====================================

How downtime slashing works:
1. Validators must sign blocks to prove they're online
2. Each validator has a 'signed_blocks_window' (e.g., 10,000 blocks)
3. Must sign min_signed_per_window (e.g., 50% = 5,000 blocks)
4. If missed > threshold, validator is jailed
5. Jailed validators cannot participate until unjailed
6. Small slash penalty applied (e.g., 0.01% of stake)
7. Validator must manually unjail themselves
